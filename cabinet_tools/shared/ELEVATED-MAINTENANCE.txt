GoldClub elevated maintenance (ConfigScanner USB)

JT25.BAT

  2) RunManteinanceTasks.1.ps1 on D__JT25\ (same mechanism as D__onlogon on field USB)

  Or create D__YourName\ and run: _RunElevated.bat D__YourName
